FAQ

Simple answers for the controlled teacher-testing version of Yellow Bird.

What is Yellow Bird?

Yellow Bird is a growing, print-first Ontario math starting library for K-6 teachers and homeschool families.

What resources are included right now?

The current library is strongest in worksheets. It also includes a smaller starter set of Quick Checks and a tiny starter set of lesson-close Exit Tickets.

What are Worksheets, Quick Checks, and Exit Tickets?

Worksheets are focused printable practice. Quick Checks are short formative checks for review, warm-up, small-group, or homeschool use. Exit Tickets are lesson-close tasks that give a quick signal after instruction.

Is this a complete curriculum?

No. Yellow Bird is a focused starting library for practice, review, and short formative checks. It does not yet cover every grade, strand, or topic.

Do I need an account?

No. You can browse, preview, download, and print the current resources without an account.

Are resources Ontario-aligned?

The library starts with Ontario math alignment and uses honest, focused skill labels. Alignment should be read as a helpful starting point, not official endorsement or full curriculum coverage.

What file formats are available?

PDF resources are available now for previewing, downloading, and printing.

Is student information required?

No. Please do not send student names, photos, or private student details in feedback.

How can I share feedback?

Use the Contact page to send controlled-testing feedback about clarity, print quality, classroom usefulness, and missing resource needs.
