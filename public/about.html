About

Project Yellow Bird is a calm, print-first Ontario math starting library for K-6 teachers and homeschool families.

What we are building

  • A strong worksheet library with a smaller starter set of Quick Checks and Exit Tickets
  • Teacher-speed browsing by grade, resource kind, domain, and practice focus
  • Preview, download, and print flow for classroom-ready practice resources
  • Careful growth based on teacher feedback and honest curriculum fit
