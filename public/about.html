About

Project Yellow Bird is a calm, print-first math resource library for K-6 teachers and homeschool families, beginning with Ontario alignment.

What we are building

  • A growing library of printable worksheets, Quick Checks, and Exit Tickets
  • Teacher-speed browsing by grade, resource kind, domain, and practice focus
  • Preview, download, and print flow for classroom-ready practice resources
  • Careful expansion based on teacher feedback and honest curriculum fit
